The Problem

Too much screen time can cause eye strain, musculoskeletal pain, sleep disruption, sedentary risks, anxiety, depression and memory issues. This can also link to bad sleeping habits as being on your phone at night can cause you to stay up late.

Our Solution

A lot of people dont actually know how many hours a day they spend on their devices. With this tracker you can get to see how much time you actually spend on screen time and help you balance your screen time. In the long term this can help lead to a healthier life without overdependence on technology. Click here to access!

Benefits

  • Put an end to tech addiction
  • Prevent eyestrain
  • Accessible
  • Record screen time data
  • Balance screen time
