Group 1

The Problem

Not getting enough sleep can cause mood changes, increased appetite and even heart disease and mental health disorders. Sleep is a stage where our body is resting to recover our energy, and it is especially important for children and teenagers as lack of sleep can be a cause for stunting, depression and low academic performance.

Our Solution

A way for people to track their sleep cleanly as a tool to improve sleeping habits. This allows people to realise the problems and patterns they have in their sleeping habits. Getting more sleep will allow you to feel refreshed and keep energy levels balanced. For teens, this can help them make sure they get adequate sleep to optimise academic performance.
