Group 2

The Problem

Unhealthy eating habits, such as skipping meals or consuming too much junk food, can lead to low energy and poor concentration throughout the day. Overtime, an unbalanced diet also weakens the immune system and increases the risk of long-term health problems.

Our Solution

A food tracker on spreadsheet that helps you keep track of your diet and caloric intake, allowing you to record what protein, carbohydrates, and fats you had for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks. It also lets you write down your mood for each meal becaues that too, is a great factor to what you're eating and lets you write down the calories you had for each meal as well as the total calories you had for the day. Benefits

  • Organised planning
  • Keeps track of your mood
  • Can track calories
  • Tracker lasts for a month
  • Includes breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks
